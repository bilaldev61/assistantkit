A page with an assistant on it

Nothing here is special. One script, one endpoint, no framework, no build step.

Try this

  • “What is available in Dubai Marina?”
  • Open a listing, then ask “how much is it?”
  • “Is the price negotiable?” — a deferral, not a guess
  • “How does renting work?” — answered from config, no model
  • “Ahmed Khan, ahmed@example.com” — becomes a lead
  • “Can you hold it for my cousin?” — filed for the team

What is happening

The widget posts to /chat. server.php hands the message to AssistantKit\Assistant with four demo ports: a fake catalogue, a JSON file per chat, a lead file, and no model at all. Everything you see comes from the rules pack.

Leads land in leads.jsonl in the temp directory — sys_get_temp_dir()/assistantkit-demo.
